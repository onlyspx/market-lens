Understanding the Table

This table displays the hourly price ranges of SPX for each trading day over the past year. Darker blue cells indicate larger price ranges within that hour. The rightmost column shows the total points gained (green) or lost (red) for each trading day.

Key Insights

  • Volatility Patterns - Identify which hours of the trading day consistently show higher volatility
  • Day Comparison - Compare volatility patterns across different days of the week
  • Opening vs. Closing Hours - Analyze differences between market open, midday, and close
  • Correlation with Results - See how early day volatility correlates with end-of-day results
