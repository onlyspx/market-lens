Understanding Market Profile

This table displays 30-minute price ranges of SPX for each trading day, labeled with Market Profile letters. Each letter represents a 30-minute period during the trading day:

  • A - 9:30 AM - 10:00 AM
  • B - 10:00 AM - 10:30 AM
  • C - 10:30 AM - 11:00 AM
  • And so on...

For each period, the table shows:

  • Range - The difference between high and low prices (darker red indicates larger range)
  • Change - The net price change within that period (green for positive, red for negative)

Key Insights

  • Market Profile Analysis - Identify which 30-minute periods consistently show higher volatility
  • Intraday Patterns - Detect recurring patterns in 30-minute periods across different days
  • Morning vs. Afternoon - Compare volatility between morning (A-F) and afternoon (G-N) periods
  • Key Time Periods - Identify which specific 30-minute blocks tend to have the most significant price movement
